Featured Projects


Seasonal Pond Measurement

This project used remotely sensed imagery of a seasonal pond in Arcata, California to determine the pond's extent at different points of the year.

  • Goal: Identify urban heat hotspots
  • Tools: ENVI, ArcGIS Pro, PlanetLabs Imagery, Excel
  • Skills: Raster analysis, data visualization, interpretation

Employment relevance: Demonstrates cartographic communication and remote sensing analysis skills.

Turbidity Visualization

A map visualizing water quality in Humboldt Bay and the nearby ocean at different times of the year.

  • Goal: Concisely visualize water quality at different periods of time
  • Tools: ENVI, ESA SNAP, ArcGIS Pro, Sentinel-2 Imagery
  • Skills: Raster analysis, data visualization, water quality analysis, interpretation

Employment relevance: Shows data visualization and interpretation skills.





Sea Level Rise Risk Model

A visualization of areas in Humboldt County at risk from sea level rise, with different inundation levels defined.

  • Goal: Communicate potential risks from sea level rise
  • Tools: ArcGIS Pro, Excel, DEM data
  • Skills: Spatial modeling, raster analysis, data visualization

Employment relevance: Demonstrates use of DEMs and understanding of ArcGIS Pro.
